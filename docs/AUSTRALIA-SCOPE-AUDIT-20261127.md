# Scope of the public Australian supplement

PR89 covers the 125 public sources, their three public guides and ordinary-guide
links, offline build/composition, source audits and the shared reader-file policy.
Household and rural additions retain equal access. Source count alone does not
prove equal practical usefulness or nationwide completeness.

Personal/nonprofit originals and their workflows/links are follow-up work. This
public branch does not expose their optional source cards or installer commands.
The public record manifest, all source HTML and existing release assets are
unchanged by the split. Runtime/map companions remain separately reviewed.

See [current public scope and coverage](AUSTRALIA-CONTENT.md),
[availability controls](AUSTRALIA-GUIDE-AVAILABILITY-REVIEW-20261127.md) and
[public-only entry-point review](AUSTRALIA-PUBLIC-SPLIT-REVIEW-20261127.md).

Maintainer acceptance, official downloads, full local manuals where absent,
applicable source permissions, community/professional review and actual household
or remote-trip arrangements are not established by this software contribution.
