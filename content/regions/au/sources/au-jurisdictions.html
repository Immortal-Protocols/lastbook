Australian state and territory emergency information

This library is offline reference material. It does not receive warnings, call emergency services, determine safe evacuation routes, or tell you whether a shelter is open. Source jurisdictions and retrieval dates appear on every reading. Sources from Queensland and NSW have regional contacts and legal requirements; these do not automatically apply elsewhere.

Police, fire or ambulance emergencies: 000. SES flood or storm emergency assistance: 132 500. The offline ACMA source explains when to call, mobile-network limits, and accessible alternatives. Read ACMA emergency-call guidance.

External territories

This table covers the six states, ACT and NT. It does not establish Christmas Island, Cocos (Keeling) Islands, Norfolk Island, Jervis Bay Territory or other external-territory warning and contact arrangements. Do not assume a neighbouring state contact or map covers them.
