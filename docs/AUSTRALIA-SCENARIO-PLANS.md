# Household and rural scenario plans

This follow-up adds two original planning readings to the public Australian
collection. It gives the two scenarios equal access: they are the first two choices
in both Australian Household and Australian Rural, and both appear under Prepare
and Learn. The collection now has 129 public readings: 102 official practical
readings, 16 rights records and 11 original navigation/planning records.

The household exercise covers **seven consecutive days without mains power**, with
an explicit longer-outage fallback. The rural exercise covers **a property isolated
by floods**, using a locally informed isolation duration and longer-isolation
contingency. Seven days is not imposed on the rural property or represented as a
safe waiting period. Neither plan is advice to remain during an evacuation.

| Coverage | Household without power | Rural flood isolation |
|---|---|---|
| People and care | Everyone present, medicines, storage, assistive equipment, accepted backup support | Residents, workers, visitors, care needs, absent owner and backup carers |
| Water and food | Seven daily needs/menus, pump dependency, safe water, food-safety limits | Safe accessible supplies, tanks/bores, pumps/treatment, protected food/feed |
| Power and essential systems | Verified equipment duration, charging, lighting, temperature, sanitation | Water/wastewater pumps, gates, essential farm tasks, verified power/fuel limits |
| Animals | Pets, food/water and relocation | Livestock and pets, early movement, holding areas, species-specific needs and safe inspection |
| Communications | Radio, phones, network/power limitations, missed-contact action | Warning sources, off-property contact, coverage alternatives and assistance requests |
| Timing and action | Seven-day log, earlier essential limits, suitable relocation and day-eight fallback | Property-specific duration, early departure before access closes, no assumed resupply |
| Recovery | Food/water checks, damaged equipment, replenish and review | Actual all-clear/road status, professional system checks, welfare and replenishment |

Each plan links to complete existing offline readings for the factual guidance.
Those source words, source dates, quantities and regional conditions are unchanged.
The new records are original contributor-authored prompts under CC BY 4.0, clearly
separated from government material. No newly acquired third-party body, private
original, PDF, personal plan or permission grant is included.

The worksheets ask for actual need, available capacity, evidence, responsibility,
an earlier decision point and a fallback. They do not supply universal fuel/water
allowances, medical treatment, generator sizing, wiring instructions or improvised
wastewater repairs. People complete their plan separately, with appropriate local,
care and equipment advice. The app does not save worksheet entries.

These are static references. Actual warnings, closures, water notices, evacuation
destinations, support commitments and system suitability must be checked for the
person and property. Offline map features do not prove safe/open roads. No authority
endorsement, professional assessment or guaranteed safe stay is claimed.
