# Dated infant CPR demonstration: source-status review

Checked 26 November 2026. This is source curation, not a replacement clinical
protocol or independent clinical approval.

The stored SCHN infant video, published in October 2025, demonstrates a two-finger
compression method. Its captions and an actual decoded frame at 100 seconds were
checked. The current [ANZCOR Guideline 6](https://www.anzcor.org/home/basic-life-support/guideline-6-compressions)
instead recommends the two-thumb encircling technique, with a one-hand alternative
when needed. The SCHN written factsheet also retains a different description.
These source variations need qualified review; this audit does not assert that
every older demonstration is universally unsafe.

The Australian health guide now calls its child/baby videos dated demonstrations.
The infant reading card and choice hint identify the comparison, check date and
pending clinical review. The original Australian source hub contains the same
status note and the primary comparison URL. The source video remains unchanged
and available as a dated source. No newly authored treatment algorithm, edited
video or clinical PDF is introduced.

An exact-file investigation also acquired three AWFA posters linked from the
publisher's current [chart index](https://www.australiawidefirstaid.com.au/resources/charts).
All three single-page originals were rendered and inspected: adult CPR, all-ages
DRSABCD and EpiPen usage. Their hashes and URLs are in the source audit. The
[general BY-ND website policy](https://www.australiawidefirstaid.com.au/resources/using-our-content)
is an unchanged-resource route, subject to exact-file and third-party conditions;
it is not a blanket prohibition on redistribution. None of the candidate PDFs,
rendered images or extracted clinical bodies is bundled in this contribution.

The adult compression-depth question was resolved against ANZCOR's actual text:
its own guidance relates approximate one-third depth to its centimetre guidance.
Those descriptions alone are not classified as a conflict. The all-ages poster's
infant technique remains a substantive comparison item. The EpiPen usage poster
also needs to be assessed as a device-use resource rather than treated as a
complete anaphylaxis plan. Rights clearance and clinical completeness are
separate decisions.

The public collection remains 108 records and the private review 429. No guide
references or clinical source files are removed. Existing household/rural
additions remain equally available. The status note is original contributor
wording in the existing source hub; source terms do not grant public reuse of
ANZCOR or SCHN bodies. The prepared ARC enquiry remains unsent pending explicit
approval. No permission or endorsement is claimed.

`content/regions/au/INFANT-CPR-SOURCE-REVIEW-20261126.json` records the primary raw
response hashes, stored-video identity, three exact PDF identities, findings and
scope. Original source files and the decoded frame remain local research only.
Generation, publication, retrieval and clinical-review dates remain distinct.

The review pack's hub note is data. The health guide's card/hint is a bundled
content asset, so an older application needs matching updated source/runtime to
show it. The data-only supplement cannot update an old executable or its bundled
guide JSON. Existing Han-search/HTTP-reader requirements also remain.
