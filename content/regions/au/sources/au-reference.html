Australia: household, rural and remote emergency reference

This library is offline reference material. It does not receive warnings, call emergency services, determine safe evacuation routes, or tell you whether a shelter is open. Source jurisdictions and retrieval dates appear on every reading. Sources from Queensland and NSW have regional contacts and legal requirements; these do not automatically apply elsewhere.

Coverage still needed

Most detailed state and territory manuals outside NSW and Queensland, route-specific remote driving and water/fuel calculations, current publicly redistributable Australian first aid, broader electrical and gas safety, disability-specific planning, translated material, and external-territory arrangements still need licensed source content and review. The included Victorian generator-usage section and national remote-travel basics address part of these gaps; they are not comprehensive manuals. This is a reviewed starting collection, not a claim of complete Australian survival coverage.

Maps are separate. The separate map proposal now includes capital boxes and eight broader state/territory extract envelopes. This text supplement contains no maps and does not establish complete geographic coverage. Place names and mapped help points are incomplete volunteered data, and do not establish facility opening, capability, evacuation status or safe access.
