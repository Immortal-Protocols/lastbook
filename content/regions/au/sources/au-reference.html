Australia: household, rural and remote emergency reference

This library is offline reference material. It does not receive warnings, call emergency services, determine safe evacuation routes, or tell you whether a shelter is open. Source jurisdictions and retrieval dates appear on every reading. Sources from Queensland and NSW have regional contacts and legal requirements; these do not automatically apply elsewhere.

Coverage still needed

Detailed state and territory guides outside NSW and Queensland, remote driving and water planning, current Australian snakebite and marine-stinger first aid, generator and electrical safety, disability-specific planning, translated material, and external-territory arrangements still need licensed source content and review. This is a reviewed starting collection, not a claim of complete Australian survival coverage.

Maps are separate. The capital-area review maps cover metropolitan boxes; they are not state-wide or nationwide maps. Place names and mapped help points are incomplete volunteered data, and do not establish facility opening, capability, evacuation status or safe access.
