Dated infant CPR source status

Checked 26 November 2026: the stored Sydney Children’s Hospitals Network infant CPR video is a 2025 source. Its infant compression demonstration differs from the method recommended in the checked ANZCOR Guideline 6. The Australian health guide flags this source variation for qualified clinical review. The publisher video remains unchanged; this note does not create a replacement treatment protocol. Use current accredited training and follow emergency-service instructions.

Comparison source: https://www.anzcor.org/home/basic-life-support/guideline-6-compressions . Source/retrieval dates are not evidence of independent clinical review. The proposed original first-aid posters and restricted ANZCOR guideline bodies are not included in this public collection.

Coverage still needed

Most detailed state and territory manuals outside NSW and Queensland, route-specific remote driving and water/fuel calculations, current publicly redistributable Australian first aid, broader electrical and gas safety, disability-specific planning, translated material, and detailed territory operating manuals still need licensed source content and review. The included Victorian generator-usage section and national remote-travel basics address part of these gaps; they are not comprehensive manuals. This is a reviewed starting collection, not a claim of complete Australian survival coverage.

Maps are separate. The separate map proposal now includes eight capital boxes, eight broader state/territory extracts and six external-territory extracts. The Australian Antarctic Territory has no established map. This text supplement contains no maps and does not establish complete geographic coverage. Place names and mapped help points are incomplete volunteered data, and do not establish facility opening, capability, evacuation status or safe access.
