Preparing for cyclones and floods

Nint must mayalmathan keengk keengk wunt pian a aak kaap’man

Ma kerrkan awuch a business nungkeram anang:

  • Ma’yalmathan emergency kit thakan
  • Ma’yalmathan Ku a ku’waak nungkeram an
  • Thowan pamwanch nungkerang thinth an wunthan
  • Regist’iman weather warning aak
  • Radio an ngeeyan

www.qld.gov.au/DisasterHelp @RecoveryQLD 1800 173 349
