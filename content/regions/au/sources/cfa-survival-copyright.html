CFA survival guide: source rights, edition and contacts

The following is the written copyright, edition and contact text from source page 20 of Your Guide to Survival, Version 6, July 2022. The exact PDF linked by CFA’s current Backup plans page was retrieved on 26 November 2026: https://www.cfa.vic.gov.au/ArticleDocuments/6528/your-guide-to-survival.pdf.aspx . Its visible edition date remains July 2022. A different older URL returns different bytes; this adaptation uses the currently linked file.

The four practical readings retain written body text, checklists and labelled table cells from source pages 2-19. Images, photographs, maps, diagrams, logos, branding, cover slogan and graphic-only labels are excluded. Property annotation text and risk/district labels are displayed as lists. No original PDF is redistributed. The adaptations add source-page headings, navigation and explicit edition/current-source notes, and correct text extraction errors against rendered pages. They do not relicense excluded material or imply agency endorsement.

Fire risk, decisions and property preparation (pages 2-7). Preparation, kit and leaving checklists (pages 8-10). Warning information (pages 11-13). During a fire and last-resort warnings (pages 14-19).
