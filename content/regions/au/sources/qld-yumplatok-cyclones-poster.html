Yumplatok: preparing for cyclones and floods

Published Yumplatok (Torres Strait Creole) poster wording from Queensland. Text-only copy; original artwork is excluded. Source dates, attribution and scope notes follow the poster text.

Preparing for Cyclones and Floods

Pamle upla sabe wanem po mekem ip i go gad cyclone or flood?

Meke sur home ane business blo yu i redi:

  • Prepare wan emergency kit
  • Meke wan evacuation plan po pamle/pets blo yu
  • Sek weya ol neighbours blo yu
  • Teke notice ane register po dem weather warnings
  • Lesen po radio

www.qld.gov.au/DisasterHelp @RecoveryQLD 1800 173 349
