Storms can happen anywhere at any time of year, and when they’re severe they can cause major damage. 

For emergency storm assistance, call 132 500. For life-threatening emergencies, call Triple Zero (000).

Steps you can take to prepare for storms

  • Secure loose items around your yard.
  • Check & clear your gutters, downpipes and drains to prevent blockages.
  • Trim trees and branches that could fall on your home or property.
  • Fix any damage to your roof.
  • Check your insurance policy is current and covers you for storm damage.

If you come across a downed powerline, stay far away and report it to SA Power Networks immediately on 13 13 66.

 

Stay informed

  • Monitor SES warnings and SES social media (more social media links at bottom of page), the SA Bureau of Meteorology (BOM) website, local media and other reliable sources for information, updates and advice
  • Read and use the SES Storm Checklist (1,089KB PDF) 
  • Read the SES Storm Brochure (1,167KB PDF) 
  • Read the SES Storms Easy English Guide (3,363KB PDF)
  • Read SES storm resources in languages other than English
  • Learn about road closures in SA metropolitan areas and road closures in the SA outback
  • Make sure you have a copy of the SES Emergency Contacts Phone List (203KB PDF)
  • Check for current SA power outages.

Look after yourself and others

  • Have your Emergency Kit (238KB PDF) ready.
  • Stay indoors and away from windows.
  • Never drive, ride or walk through floodwater.
  • Find safe shelter if outdoors.
  • Bring pets and animals inside to keep them safe.
  • Check on your neighbours.
  • Be careful when driving as you may not be able to see very well and trees and branches may have fallen across the road.
  • Stay well clear of fallen powerlines and report them to SA Power Networks on 13 13 66.

If a storm warning is issued

  • Call 132 500 for emergency assistance. For life-threatening emergencies, call Triple Zero (000)
  • Activate your Emergency Plan (108KB PDF)
  • Locate your Emergency Kit (238KB PDF)
  • Make sure you understand SES warnings and follow the advice of SES current warnings
  • Monitor SES warnings and SES social media (more social media links at bottom of page), the SA BOM website, local media and other reliable sources for information, updates and advice.
  • Visit the SES How to lay sandbags webpage to learn how to minimise the impact of flooding on your property.
  • Place sandbags in appropriate places in and around your home.
