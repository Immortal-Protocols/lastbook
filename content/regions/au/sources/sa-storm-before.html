Storms can happen anywhere at any time of year, and when they’re severe they can cause major damage. 

For emergency storm assistance, call 132 500. For life-threatening emergencies, call Triple Zero (000).

Steps you can take to prepare for storms

  • Secure loose items around your yard.
  • Check & clear your gutters, downpipes and drains to prevent blockages.
  • Trim trees and branches that could fall on your home or property.
  • Fix any damage to your roof.
  • Check your insurance policy is current and covers you for storm damage.

Get informed

  • Read and use the SES Storm Checklist (1,089KB PDF)
  • Read the SES Storm Brochure (1,167KB PDF)
  • Read the SES Storms Easy English Guide (3,363KB PDF).
  • Read SES storm resources in languages other than English.
  • Visit the SES How to lay sandbags webpage to learn how to minimise the impact of flooding on your property.
  • Monitor SES warnings and SES social media (more social media links at bottom of page), the SA Bureau of Meteorology website, local media and other reliable sources for information, updates and advice.
  • Download, print and keep a copy of the SES Emergency Contacts Phone List (203KB PDF).

Get ready

  • Check your insurance policy is current and covers you for storm damage.
  • Make sure you know when to call 132 500.
  • Clear your gutters, downpipes and drains.
  • Fix any damage to your roof.
  • Secure all loose items around your house.
  • Park your car under cover and well away from trees.
  • Trim trees and branches that could fall on your home or property. If there are trees or powerlines that may pose a threat to your property, contact your council or energy company to check them.
  • Buy a battery-powered radio and know your emergency radio frequencies.
  • Get to know your neighbours as they may need a hand getting ready for the storm.
  • Download and use the Red Cross Get Prepared app on iOS and Android.

Be prepared for emergencies

  • Download and use the SES Home Emergency Plan (107KB PDF).
  • Download and use the SES Emergency Kit Checklist (238KB PDF) to list all the things you may need in an emergency.
  • Keep mobile phones fully charged and have a battery-powered radio and torch ready in case of a power outage.
