Australian state and territory emergency information

Lastbook is offline reference material. It cannot receive warnings, place calls or confirm that a service, road or shelter is available.

This directory covers the six states, ACT, Northern Territory, Jervis Bay Territory and all seven external territories. Coverage means dated contact or management references, not complete local emergency manuals. Jervis Bay is an internal territory; it is not an external territory or part of the ACT. Neighbouring maps and state laws do not establish local contact arrangements.

ACMA: emergency calls, mobile-network limits and power outages. Accessible emergency calling: TTY and SMS Relay are different services.

Numbers below are labelled for the jurisdiction and service supported by the source. The six-state and ACT contacts retain their 17 November 2026 check date. NT contacts were rechecked on 26 November 2026. The eight added territory entries were checked on 26 November 2026. Older source publication dates remain visible; retrieval is not an update or live service test.

Online addresses are written references for use outside Lastbook when connected. This page does not open or retrieve them.
