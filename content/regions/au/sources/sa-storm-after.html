Storms can happen anywhere at any time of year, and when they’re severe they can cause major damage. 

For emergency storm assistance, call 132 500. For life-threatening emergencies, call Triple Zero (000).

Steps you can take to prepare for storms

  • Secure loose items around your yard.
  • Check & clear your gutters, downpipes and drains to prevent blockages.
  • Trim trees and branches that could fall on your home or property.
  • Fix any damage to your roof.
  • Check your insurance policy is current and covers you for storm damage.

If you come across a downed powerline, stay far away and report it to SA Power Networks immediately on 13 13 66.

 

Keep in mind

  • Check your home and property for damage
  • Keep clear of damaged buildings, powerlines and trees
  • Be aware of road hazards, such as floodwater, debris, and damaged roads and bridges
  • Don't drive through affected areas unless it is necessary
  • Don't let children play in or near floodwaters caused by the storm
  • Never drive, ride or walk through floodwaters caused by the storm
  • Check on your neighbours when it's safe to do so
  • Continue to monitor SES warnings and SES social media (more social media links at bottom of page), the SA Bureau of Meteorology website, local media and other reliable sources for information, updates and advice.
